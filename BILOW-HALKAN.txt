================================================================

Nidaamku waa HAL SALDHIG: Saldhigga Ciidanka Booliska Gobolka Gabiley.

1. Bilow Apache iyo MySQL (XAMPP/Laragon/WAMP).
2. Folder-ka geli htdocs, tusaale:
      C:\xampp\htdocs\ciidanka
3. Fur:
      http://localhost/ciidanka/install.php
4. Geli xogta MySQL; XAMPP badanaa:
      Host: localhost
      Database: gabiley_police
      Username: root
      Password: madhan
5. Buuxi akoonka maamulaha koowaad: magac buuxa, email iyo password.
   (Ugu yaraan 10 xaraf, xaraf weyn, xaraf yar iyo tiro.)
6. Guji Rakib Nidaamka.
7. Marka uu dhammaado TIRTIR install.php.
8. Fur login.html oo gal adigoo isticmaalaya akoonkaas.

AKOONKA KOOWAAD
---------------
Password wadaagsan oo hore loo sameeyay MA JIRO. Akoonka Super Admin
waxaad adigu dhigaysaa marka aad rakibayso.

Shaqaalaha kale: Users & Permissions -> Email ugu Casuum Shaqaale.
Qofkaasi wuxuu password-kiisa ka sameystaa link ammaan ah.

Database-ku wuxuu ku bilaabmaa MADHAN — xog tijaabo ah lama gelin.
