================================================================
 EMAIL INVITATIONS + RUKHSADAHA SHAQAALAHA
================================================================

A) HADDII DATABASE-KU CUSUB YAHAY
- install.php ayaa si toos ah u sameynaya user_permissions iyo user_invitations.

B) HADDII DATABASE-KU HORE U JIRAY
1. Backup samee.
2. Fur browser-ka:
   http://localhost/ciidanka/upgrade-access.php
3. Guji "Orod Upgrade-ka Hal Mar".
4. Marka uu guulaysto, TIRTIR upgrade-access.php.

C) SI EMAIL DHAB AH LOO DIRO
1. Nuqul ka samee:
   config/mail.local.example.php
2. Magaca nuqulka ka dhig:
   config/mail.local.php
3. Geli SMTP-gaaga: host, port, username, password/app-password,
   from_email iyo app_base_url.
4. enabled => true ka dhig.
5. app_base_url sax: haddii staff-ka PC/telefoon kale ka galayo, HA isticmaalin localhost;
   isticmaal IP/URL ay gaadhi karaan (tusaale http://192.168.1.50/ciidanka) ama HTTPS domain.
6. Gmail badanaa: smtp.gmail.com, port 587, encryption tls,
   waxaana la isticmaalaa App Password haddii account-ku sidaas u baahan yahay.

Haddii SMTP aan weli la dejin:
- Taliyuhu wali wuu abuuri karaa account-ka iyo rukhsadaha.
- System-ku wuxuu siinayaa invitation link la koobi-gareyn karo.
- Shaqaalaha link-gaas ayuu ka sameysan karaa password.

D) HABKA SHAQAALAHA
1. Taliyaha -> Users & Permissions.
2. "+ Email ugu Casuum Shaqaale".
3. Geli magaca, email, role iyo clearance.
4. Calaamadee QAYBAHA uu arki karo.
5. Calaamadee FICILLADA uu samayn karo.
6. Dir casuumaadda.
7. Shaqaaluhu email/link ayuu ka sameystaa password.
8. Markuu galo, wuxuu arkaa oo keliya wixii loo oggolaaday.
9. Haddii Taliyuhu rukhsad ka beddelo, session-kiisa waa la xiraa oo waa inuu dib u login sameeyaa.

AMNI:
- Sidebar keliya lama qariyo; API-ga server-ka ayaa sidoo kale 403 ku diidaya qaybaha/actions-ka aan la oggolaan.
- Shaqaale aan weli individual permission loo qoondeyn wuxuu arkaa Dashboard + Profile oo keliya (fail-closed).
- Marka Taliyuhu rukhsad beddelo, sessions-kii shaqaalaha waa la revoke-gareeyaa,
  wuxuuna ku qasbanaanayaa inuu dib u login sameeyo.
